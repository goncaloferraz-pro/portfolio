# Gunther Eisen (Gonçalo Ferraz) — UX Portfolio

UX practice since 2004. I work end-to-end — research, information architecture, interaction design, and the design QA that gets work shipped — helping product teams understand what users actually need, and why the current experience falls short of that.

I have led engagements at principal level for B2B SaaS platforms, legal systems, web hosting products, health tech, and financial services — working embedded with product teams and as an independent consultant.
